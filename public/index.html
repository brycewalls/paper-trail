How a session goes

  1. Grab a stack of about 20 documents and set a 20-minute timer.
  2. Scan each one in the Files app (the ⋯ menu, then Scan Documents) into one folder, one PDF per document. Or photograph it flat in good light.
  3. Choose the whole folder of PDFs at once. Swipe through the deck while Claude reads ahead, and put each paper on the pile its stamp says.
  4. At the end, open the Shred pile tab, confirm each scan opens in Drive, shred, and mark it shredded.
What always stays on paper

Birth, marriage, divorce and death certificates, Social Security card, passport, car titles, deeds, wills, trusts, powers of attorney, anything notarized or with a raised seal, and original stock or bond certificates. Scan them for reference, then keep them together in one folder or a fireproof pouch.
