Paper Trail for iPhone: three directions

Low-fi sketches at iPhone 15 Pro Max proportions (393 x 852 pt). Same app, same data, three different answers to how sorting a box of paperwork should feel. All names, senders and amounts are made up.

A

Glass: native and calm

Feels like an Apple app. System grouped backgrounds, a floating Liquid Glass tab bar with a search island, the outbox as a tab bar accessory (like the Music mini player), sheets with detents for anything that needs a decision. One big card at a time, medium detail.

B

Desk: tactile and paper-like

The web app's rubber stamps, grown into a whole world. The scan itself is the card, with a manila tag clipped on. You drag it into a shredder slot or a Drive tray. Piles are real stacks like the app icon, the outbox is an out tray of envelopes, the tracker is a ledger. Folder tabs instead of a tab bar. Least on the card at once.

C

Console: dense and fast

Built for clearing a whole box in one sitting. A split inbox (Check / Confident / Waiting) with one-tap bulk filing for confident cards, a compact card with every field and a confidence meter, a four-button triage bar, and short and long swipes on list rows. Tables over illustrations. Most on screen at once.
